ClipNode in one HTML file.

This demo loads the package from jsDelivr, boots the worklet from the CDN, synthesizes its own stereo clip, and foregrounds the things AudioBufferSourceNode does not give you: pause/resume, reusable start, buffer hot-swap, loop callbacks, loop crossfade, real-time playhead control, and sample-accurate fades.

What you are hearing

A short synthetic groove with bass, hats, and melodic stabs rendered into an AudioBuffer, then played through ClipNode with loop telemetry and direct playhead control.
